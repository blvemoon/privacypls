Sunrise Bakery Privacy Policy

We keep this simple because we only collect what we need to take your orders.

What we collect: When you place an online order, we collect your name, email address, and the items you ordered. We do not collect payment card numbers; payments are handled by our payment processor.

How we use it: We use your information only to prepare your order and send you an order confirmation. If you sign up for our newsletter, we use your email address to send it.

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Cookies: Our website uses one cookie to keep items in your shopping cart. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion: We delete order records 30 days after your order is picked up. You can ask us to delete your information at any time by emailing hello@sunrisebakery.example, and we will delete it within 30 days.

Newsletter: Every newsletter includes a one-click unsubscribe link.

Changes: If we change this policy, we will email customers who have ordered in the past year at least 30 days before the change takes effect.
