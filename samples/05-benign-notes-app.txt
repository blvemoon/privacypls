QuietNotes Privacy Policy

QuietNotes is a note-taking app that stores your notes only on your own device.

We do not collect any personal information. We do not have accounts, and we cannot see your notes.

The app does not contain analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind, and it makes no network requests except to check for updates, which sends only the app version number.

If you choose to back up your notes, they are encrypted on your device before they are saved to the cloud storage service you pick. We cannot read your backups.

You can delete all your data at any time by deleting the app.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be shown in the app and you will be asked to accept it before continuing.

Questions? Email privacy@quietnotes.example.
