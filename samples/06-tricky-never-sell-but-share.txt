BrightCart Privacy Policy

Our promise: We never sell your personal information.

What we collect: your name, shipping address, email, order history, and the pages you browse on our site.

How we share information: We share your email address, order history, and browsing activity with our advertising partners so they can show you personalized ads on other websites and social media. We also share information with service providers who help us ship orders and process payments; they may only use it to provide those services to us.

Your choices: You can opt out of personalized advertising at any time with one click using the "Do not share my information" link at the bottom of every page. You can delete your account in your account settings, and we will delete your data within 30 days.

Retention: We keep order records for 7 years to meet tax laws, and delete browsing data after 12 months.
